# Wistron PM Chinese

A single-page study tool for workplace Mandarin (Traditional Chinese, Taiwan usage) for a product manager role in Taiwan's server/electronics industry.

## What's inside

- **詞彙 Vocabulary** – 126 terms in eight groups: org & roles, development, cost & quality, communication, factory & production, data & statistics, office life, and spoken Taiwan expressions. Each has pinyin, English and a usage note. Hide pinyin or English to self-test, and star words for the quiz.
- **中英夾雜 Mixed** – 24 English words Taiwan tech offices drop into Chinese (issue, follow, sync, hold, cue, loading…), each in a real sentence.
- **句型 Phrases** – ready-to-use sentences for meetings, email/Teams, everyday talk, your first week, quick chat replies, phone calls and presenting data.
- **職務內容 Your role** – four typical PM duties explained word by word, plus a self-introduction template.
- **測驗 Quiz** – multiple choice in three modes: Chinese → English, English → Chinese, Chinese → pinyin.
- **職場提示 Tips** – Taiwan office habits such as mixing in English acronyms, 麻煩, 辛苦了, and Traditional vs. Simplified differences, plus a Taiwan vs. mainland word list.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser. No install or build step.
Pronunciation uses your browser's built-in Chinese (zh-TW) voice where available. Starred words are saved in your browser.
